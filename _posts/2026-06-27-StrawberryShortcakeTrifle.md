## Ingredients

### Vanilla Cake

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.25 tsp baking soda
- 2.75 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1.5 cups unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 egg whites
- 3 tsp vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups milk

### Strawberry Sauce

- 1 lb strawberries, pureed
- 0.5 cups sugar
- 0.75 tsp vanilla extract

### Whipped Cream & Assembly

- 2.75 cups heavy whipping cream, cold
- 1.5 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 2 lbs strawberries, chopped

## Steps

### Vanilla Cake

1. Preheat oven to 350° F. Grease a 9x13 pan.
2. Combine **flour**, **baking soda**, **baking powder**, and **salt** in a bowl.
   Set aside.
3. Beat **butter** and **sugar** on medium for 2 minutes, until light and fluffy.
   Scrape down the sides of the bowl.
4. Add **egg whites** and **vanilla**, beat on medium until smooth and well
   combined.
5. Add a third of the flour mixture, beat until incorporated.
6. Add half the **milk**, beat until incorporated.
7. Add another third of the flour mixture, beat until incorporated, scraping the
   sides as needed.
8. Add the remaining milk, beat on medium until incorporated.
9. Add the remaining flour mixture, beat until well combined, scraping as needed.
10. Bake 35-40 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
    Let cool.

### Strawberry Sauce

1. Add pureed **strawberries**, **sugar**, and **vanilla** to a medium saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat until it begins to boil, stirring occasionally.
3. Boil for 1 minute, then remove from heat and set aside to cool.

### Whipped Cream & Assembly

1. Add **heavy whipping cream**, **powdered sugar**, and **vanilla** to a mixer
   bowl. Whip on high until stiff peaks form.
2. Layer in a trifle dish: cake, half the chopped **strawberries**, half the
   strawberry sauce, and half the whipped cream.
3. Add a second layer of cake, strawberries, and sauce. Pipe the remaining
   whipped cream on top in swirls and garnish with additional strawberries.
